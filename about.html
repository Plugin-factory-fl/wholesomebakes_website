Laura Miller, Founder

[Placeholder bio — replace with Laura's story]

Laura Miller founded Wholesome Bakes with a simple mission: prove that gluten-free baking can be every bit as indulgent, beautiful, and satisfying as traditional favorites. After years of experimenting in her home kitchen, she began sharing her creations with friends and neighbors across St. Augustine.

Today, Laura specializes in custom orders, celebration cakes, and everyday treats that bring joy to families navigating gluten-free lifestyles. She sources quality ingredients, tests every recipe until it's just right, and treats every order like it's headed to her own table.

When she's not baking, you'll find Laura at local markets, community events, and pop-ups throughout the St. Augustine area — always ready with a warm smile and a sample of something sweet.
